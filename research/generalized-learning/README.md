# Rights-scoped generalized learning experiment

[Codito #69](https://github.com/Entif-AI/Codito/issues/69) owns these finite
synthetic fixtures. Public representation belongs to Rosetta #1504. Topology,
trust and proof admission remain under Rosetta #84/#96/#759, and privacy/erasure
under their existing owners. No Codito shape becomes an official interchange
contract, signed proof, policy, procedural-memory record or swarm network.

Run `python -m codito.generalized_learning`. Four families cover computer use,
personal assistance, collective retail privacy stress and agentic engineering.
Their source episodes are authored synthetic private markers, not captured
screens, audio, timelines, identities, customer data or private code. Source
bytes/local IDs remain separate from interpretation and export. The published
fixture source is reproducible experiment material, not real private telemetry.

Safe-path candidates use a closed, deliberately conventional rule vocabulary.
They carry only declared scope/version/purpose, opaque experimental evidence
roots and challenge/supersession state. The experiment consumes supplied consent,
rights, sensitivity, third-party and local-validation facts. It cannot infer
those facts or issue authorization. Conflicting valid lessons preserve dissent
and produce no export; copied roots never count as independent support.

The explicit experimental profile requests two independent source roots. This
is a toy support treatment, not an anonymity threshold, privacy budget, Entif
promotion rule or signer admission policy. IDs/digests establish identity only.
Real provenance, rights and trustworthy validation require the owning Rosetta
contracts. The fixture is capable of refusing export and retaining zero lesson.

Six controls per family compare local-only, a deliberately unsafe naive summary,
scoped rights-gated export, correlated copies, stale recipient version and a
low-quality contributor. The naive "abstraction" retains a synthetic local marker
and lacks provenance/scope. Its exposure is deliberately measured as harm.
Recipients can accept locally, reject scope/version/purpose or local-test failure,
retain insufficient/conflicting evidence, and supersede a previously adopted
lesson. None of these outcomes grants external effects.

Separate conventional rule predicates produce an observable bounded suggestion
from fixture state. Independent expected suggestions evaluate terminal correctness.
Local validation and later postcondition failure remain separate. A naive
transfer can produce the right suggestion while its privacy/provenance judgment
remains invalid. Joint correctness never retroactively repairs that judgment.
The rule library and supplied state carry competence; no neural-model learning
or inferred privacy decision is claimed.

An attributed local correction creates a new digest-bound revoked record and
preserves the old record. The recipient invalidates admitted derivatives, and
a correction arriving before the original prevents late resurrection. These
are in-process fixture deliveries under the experiment's assumed local sender.
They do not prove untrusted remote correction, signatures, distributed erasure
or transport durability. Public swarm implementation remains outside this work.

Metrics distinguish terminal utility, false transfer/privacy harm, raw-marker
exposure as a reconstructability proxy, independent roots, local rejection and
correction propagation. Exact serialized export/correction bytes and local CPU
nanoseconds are measured. No bytes cross a network; no provider or model runs.
Zero marker exposure proves only this fixture property, not differential privacy,
secure aggregation, anonymity or general non-reconstructability.

Engineering already knows its rule in the local-only control, so sharing can add
coordination bytes without new utility. Other families can gain a locally valid
rule without exporting raw history. Retail can refuse every scoped export.
Negative, rejected and inconclusive outcomes remain visible. The study is an
unregistered public synthetic protocol with no sealed holdout or efficacy claim.
